**Description (max 4000)**
```
Baby Care Clock helps new parents keep track of everything a little one needs, without having to remember it all. Set how often each thing happens and we'll remind you the moment it's due.

A REMINDER RIGHT ON TIME
• A notification the moment a feed, diaper change, nap or dose is due
• Tap Done or Snooze 15 min right from the notification
• Optional early heads-up 5, 15 or 30 minutes before

ONE TAP, EVEN AT 3 AM
• A big "Next up" card with a countdown ring and a large Done button
• Forgot to log it? Pick "15 min ago" or any time in the past week
• Snooze or skip a dose with a swipe
• Add a quick note like "Left side, 15 min"
• Undo anything you tap by mistake

VITAMINS AND MEDICINES
• Ready-made options: Vitamin D3, iron drops, calcium, multivitamin, probiotic
• Record the dosage so everyone gives the same amount
• Once-a-day items stay on a daily rhythm

TAP DONE FROM ANYWHERE
• Done buttons on the Home Screen widget and on each reminder
• Apple Watch app: see what's next and tap Done from your wrist during night feeds
• Siri: "Log feed in Baby Care Clock" or "What's next in Baby Care Clock"

VACCINATIONS
• The IAP vaccination schedule worked out from your baby's birth date
• Reminders 3 days before and on the day of each visit
• Mark vaccines as given and add notes. Every date can be changed to match your pediatrician's card

A CLEAR HISTORY
• A day-by-day timeline of everything logged
• Daily totals, e.g. Feed ×8, Diaper ×6
• Share a day's summary as an image or text with your partner, nanny or pediatrician

MADE FOR YOUR BABY
• Add your baby's name, age and photo
• Your baby's photo on the Home Screen widget
• Soft, calming design with full light and dark mode for night feeds

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Baby Care Clock is a reminder tool, not medical advice. Always follow your pediatrician's guidance.
```

## App Review Information
- Sign-in required: **No**
- Notes:
```
Baby Care Clock is an offline baby care reminder app. No login or network is needed. To test: complete the 4-step onboarding (enter any name; the photo is optional), choose a few items such as Feed and Vitamin D3, and allow notifications. On the Today tab, tap Done on the "Next up" card or tap any row to log it at an earlier time. Swipe a row for Snooze/Skip. History shows the day's log; Care Plan adds or edits items. To see a notification quickly, add an item in Care Plan with "Starts: Due later" set one minute ahead.
```
