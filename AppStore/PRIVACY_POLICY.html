Baby Care Clock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Baby Care Clock is a baby care reminder and logging app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Baby Care Clock does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your baby's name, birth date and optional photo, your care plan, the history of what you log, and your app preferences are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its widget. If you use the Apple Watch app, what's due next is sent directly to your paired Apple Watch. A copy is also kept in your iPhone's secure Keychain so your data can be restored if you delete and reinstall the app; it is not synced or uploaded. Your information never leaves your devices unless you choose to share a day summary or export a backup using the iOS share sheet. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone and work without an internet connection.

You can delete everything, including the Keychain copy, at any time in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes its data, but the Keychain copy stays on your iPhone so it can be restored on reinstall; on the welcome screen, choose Start fresh to delete it.

Photos

If you add a photo, the app only receives the single photo you pick. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

Not medical advice

Baby Care Clock is a reminder tool. It does not provide medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your pediatrician, pharmacist or the medicine's label.

Children

The app is used by parents and caregivers and collects no personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
