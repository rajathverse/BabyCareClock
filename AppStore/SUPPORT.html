Common questions

Where are the Done and Snooze buttons on a reminder?
Press and hold the notification (or swipe it left and tap View) to show them.

I'm not hearing reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Baby Care Clock → Notifications. If you use a Focus mode such as Sleep, add Baby Care Clock to its allowed apps.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?
No. When you reinstall, the welcome screen offers to restore your baby's data. Choose Start fresh if you'd rather begin again.

How do I delete everything?
In the app, open Settings → Delete all data.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use it).

Medical note

Baby Care Clock is a reminder tool, not medical advice. Always follow your pediatrician's guidance.
